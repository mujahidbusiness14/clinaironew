Boundaries

What Clinairo Deliberately Does Not Do

A feature list that only says yes isn't much use to you. These are the lines we've drawn on purpose.

×

No clinical advice, ever

The system does not diagnose, interpret symptoms or recommend treatment based on a medical condition. Those questions escalate to your provider with an alert. The boundary is enforced in the system, not left to a model's judgment.

×

No AI voice chasing patients

Follow-up runs over text. Anything that needs a voice goes to a person on your team with the full context. It's better for patients and keeps you on simpler ground with telemarketing rules.

×

No replacing your front desk

We take the repetitive work so your team can handle the nervous first-timer and the patient at the counter. Practices that hand everything to AI generally regret it.

×

No clinical records

We hold contact details, appointments, treatment interest and consultation outcomes. No clinical notes, no diagnoses, no treatment photography — a smaller data footprint is a real safeguard, not a limitation.
