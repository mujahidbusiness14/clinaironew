Features

Everything Your Front Desk Needs, in One AI System

Eight features working together as a single product — not a stack of disconnected tools.

📞
FEATURE 01

24/7 AI Voice Receptionist

Answers every incoming call with natural, human-like conversation — day, night, weekends, and holidays. No hold music, no voicemail, no missed patient.

📅
FEATURE 02

AI Appointment Scheduling

Checks real-time calendar availability, books, confirms, and reschedules appointments automatically, syncing instantly with your existing calendar.

📲
FEATURE 03

Missed Call Text-Back

If a call is ever missed, Clinairo instantly sends a text to re-engage the patient and offer to book — turning a lost call into a booked visit.

💬
FEATURE 04

WhatsApp Patient Support

Patients can message on WhatsApp to ask questions, book, or reschedule — answered instantly, any time of day.

🔔
FEATURE 05

Appointment Reminders

Automated SMS, WhatsApp, and email reminders sent ahead of every visit, reducing no-shows without any manual follow-up.

📋
FEATURE 06

Patient FAQ Assistant

Handles common questions — hours, pricing, insurance, location — instantly and accurately, freeing your staff from repetitive calls.

🔄
FEATURE 07

CRM Synchronization

Every call, booking, and message syncs directly into your CRM automatically, keeping patient records complete and current.

📊
FEATURE 08

Daily Operational Reports

A daily summary of calls, bookings, and missed inquiries delivered straight to the clinic owner's inbox — full visibility, zero manual reporting.
