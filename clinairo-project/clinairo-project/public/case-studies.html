The Situation

A Practice With No Way to Cover Every Call

Not a technology problem. A coverage problem — the doctor in a consultation, every assistant on another line, and the phone still ringing.

Before Clinairo

The gaps

Office hours only, with no fallback when the team was occupied.

  • When Dr. Siddique was in consultation, appointment requests couldn't be handled
  • When all assistants were simultaneously occupied, calls went unanswered
  • Zero after-hours coverage — evening and weekend callers got no response
  • Patients seeking clinic information after hours had no way to get answers
  • No fallback at all when doctor and assistants were both unavailable
After Clinairo

The system

One hybrid AI and human workflow covering every hour of the week.

  • 24/7 AI voice receptionist answering all inbound patient calls
  • Automated information delivery covering clinic details and conditions treated
  • Appointment scheduling at any hour without staff involvement
  • Seamless AI-to-human handoff connecting patients to assistants when needed
  • Always-available patient communication, with a human always reachable
