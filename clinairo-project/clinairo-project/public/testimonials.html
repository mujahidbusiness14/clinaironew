Why this page isn't longer

Because we won't fill it with names that don't exist. Earlier versions of this website carried testimonials from practices we had invented, and we took them down. If you have ever searched a vendor's testimonial and found nothing, you know exactly why that matters.

Our rule now: a real client, a real practice, written permission, and numbers they have reviewed before we publish them. It makes for a shorter page today and a defensible one in every sales conversation.
