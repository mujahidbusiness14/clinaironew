Questions & Answers

Common Inquiries

How does the AI receptionist work?+

Clinairo's AI answers incoming calls using natural voice conversation, understands the patient's request, checks your calendar, and books, reschedules, or answers questions — all without a human on the line.

Can it integrate with our scheduling system?+

Yes — Clinairo connects with common calendar and CRM platforms so bookings sync automatically without any manual double-entry.

Does it work after clinic hours?+

Yes. Clinairo runs 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays — the exact hours when clinics typically lose the most calls.

Can patients book appointments automatically?+

Yes — patients can book, reschedule, or cancel entirely through the AI, whether by phone, WhatsApp, or chat, with confirmations sent instantly.

Is the system customizable for our clinic?+

Yes. The AI's tone, knowledge base, and booking rules are configured specifically to match your clinic's services and style during onboarding.
