What's Included

Not Five Tools That Talk to Each Other. One That Already Knows.

A patient who calls at 9pm, books, no-shows, gets rescheduled by text, comes in, leaves undecided, then books four days later is one continuous conversation — not five disconnected apps your front desk reconciles by hand every morning.

AI receptionist

Answers every call in natural conversation, around the clock, with no busy signal during a promotion. Trained on your treatment menu, your providers and your policies.

  • Speaks the language — units, syringes, downtime, aftercare
  • Never gives clinical advice; medical questions route to your team
  • Handles several callers at once, so nobody hears a busy tone

Booking at your platform's capability

We confirm what your booking software actually allows before we promise anything — full write-back, event sync, calendar, or staff-assisted. You get the real answer for your plan during onboarding, not a sales claim.

  • Four honest capability tiers, assigned after we check your plan
  • Calendar and staff-assisted paths work with any practice
  • Custom integration work priced into setup, never hidden

Consult protection

Stop paying full acquisition cost for consultations that never happen. Confirmation, reminders and rescue, handled without anyone on your team remembering to do it.

  • Reminder cascade at 72 hours, 24 hours and 3 hours
  • Intake collected before arrival, so the provider walks in prepared
  • No-show rescue within minutes of a missed slot

Consult recovery

Your provider taps one button after the consultation — booked, thinking, price, or timing. That answer starts a 21-day text sequence written for that specific objection, referencing the treatment she discussed and the provider she saw.

  • Stops the second she replies; your team only sees warm conversations
  • Text only — anything needing a voice goes to a human
  • Never opens with a discount, which just teaches patients to stall

Human review queue

Anything that needs a person lands in one place with the full conversation, the reason it escalated, and a recommended next step.

  • Medical questions, complaints and urgent concerns escalate instantly
  • High-value leads flagged for a human before they go cold
  • One-click handoff, with context already written for you

Revenue attribution

Consultation, outcome, follow-up, booking, revenue — one traceable chain, measured against the baseline we record before we start. Recovered revenue becomes a number you can audit rather than a claim you have to trust.

  • Baseline period measured before any changes go live
  • Influenced, recovered, booked and collected reported separately
  • Monthly report puts one number next to our invoice
